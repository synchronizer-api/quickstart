# Case Study: Dentina

Dentina is an AI-powered virtual dental receptionist that answers calls, talks to patients, and books appointments 24/7.

---

## The Challenge
- Conflicting data across Dentrix, Eaglesoft, and Open Dental  
- Double-booked and “phantom” appointment slots  
- Heavy manual reconciliation by staff  
- Difficulty onboarding new practices quickly  

---

## The Solution
Synchronizer was deployed as a single API layer to normalize data across PMS platforms.  
This eliminated fragile one-off integrations and gave Dentina a single source of truth for appointments, patients, and scheduling.  

The Synchronizer team worked directly with Dentina’s leadership and developers to guide integration, troubleshoot early challenges, and ensure a smooth rollout.  

---

## Results
- 2 Months to Build and Launch
- 40% calls automated
- 100% reduction in duplicate patient records  
- Faster onboarding for new practices  
- Streamlined scheduling across Dentrix, Eaglesoft, and Open Dental  
- A scalable foundation for future integrations  

---

## Why It Mattered
Dentina’s founder is both a practicing dentist and a software engineer. That dual perspective highlighted the need for an integration layer built for **clinical realities** and **developer workflows**.  
Synchronizer provided the support and infrastructure to make that vision a reality.
