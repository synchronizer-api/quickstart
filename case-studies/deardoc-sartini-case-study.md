# Sartini Dentistry × DearDoc × NexHealth Synchronizer  
**60+ hours saved monthly through automated scheduling + intake**

Sartini General Dentistry needed a way for patients to book and complete intake outside of limited office hours. With the front desk closing at **2pm**, calls stretched to 30 minutes, paperwork required cleanup, and last-minute openings were difficult to fill.

## The Fix: Synchronizer as the Data Layer  
The **Synchronizer API** became the foundation for every workflow improvement:

- **Real-time availability** — all systems read/write from a single source of truth  
- **Consistent patient + intake data** — bookings at 9pm flow directly into the PMS  
- **Cross-system normalization** — no manual corrections across DearDoc, NexHealth, and the PMS  

With clean, real-time data, DearDoc added:

- AI-powered website chat  
- Missed-call AI + Autodialer  
- Automated Google review reminders  

The result: a unified experience from website → intake → appointment → follow-up.

## Results  
- **60+ admin hours saved per month**  
- **50% of patients book online**, mostly after hours  
- **Hundreds of Google reviews** from automated post-visit texts  
- Faster intake, fewer errors, smoother patient flow  

> “We close at 2pm. Patients still want to schedule at 5pm, 7pm or 9pm. Now they can do it on their own time.”

- Real-time scheduling without polling hacks
